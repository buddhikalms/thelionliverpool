Our Story

The Story

Our restaurant specialises in both Japanese and Sri Lankan cuisine. It is a fusion of flavours from our life. The array of enchanting spices from the roots of Sri Lanka intertwined with the delicate art of Japanese cuisine, all available on our menu. From me and my wife’s time in Japan and our mother country’s cuisine. My brother-in-law and I began our journey of opening this restaurant during the pandemic, facing lots of hardships and delays due to restrictions. Of course now I can see that I do not regret the journey we have taken as a family to be able to create a community here at The Lion, where we offer exceptional customer service and even better food. Now, when I see what this restaurant has become, I see a place where people can come together and create memories and the idea that my food can help them do that… That is what reminds me why this business was started.

Our Commitment

At Lion Restaurant, our passion is to create an unforgettable dining experience that honors the culinary essence of Sri Lanka and Japan. Our commitment to authenticity, quality, and hospitality is the cornerstone of our service. We source only the finest ingredients, ensuring each dish is not only a feast for the palate but also a tribute to the rich culinary traditions of these two vibrant cultures.
Our skilled chefs blend time-honored recipes with innovative techniques to bring you a unique fusion of flavors. At Lion, every guest is treated to the warmth of Sri Lankan hospitality and the meticulous attention to detail synonymous with Japanese cuisine. We’re dedicated to sustainability, community engagement, and delivering exceptional dining experiences to our guests in Liverpool.
